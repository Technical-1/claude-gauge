claude-usage — icon directions

Eight standalone variants. Each is live HTML/SVG rendered at real size, not a downscaled bitmap — so the 32 and 64 columns show exactly what macOS will draw. The small columns are where these get decided.

INSTRUMENT, CREAM

01-instrument-cream.html

Personality: Vintage pressure gauge. Machined, warm, physical.
Anti-goal: A flat dashboard widget. Anything that looks drawn in Figma defaults.

INSTRUMENT, THREE NEEDLES

02-instrument-three-needles.html

Personality: The same machined dial, carrying three readings at once.
Anti-goal: A clock. Three hands on a round face is a very short trip to one.

INSTRUMENT, BRASS

03-instrument-brass.html

Personality: Aviation instrument. Brass ring, black face, lit dial.
Anti-goal: The current icon — dark elements on a dark ground with nothing to grip.

WARM MINIMAL

04-warm-minimal.html

Personality: Confident and quiet. A single gesture in Claude's terracotta.
Anti-goal: A generic progress ring. The dot and the 280-degree sweep are what stop it.

WARM, THREE ARCS

05-warm-three-arcs.html

Personality: The multi-account idea as the mark itself.
Anti-goal: Activity rings. Different sweep, different weights, open at the bottom.

SPLIT DIAL

06-split-dial.html

Personality: An instrument rebuilt around three readings instead of one.
Anti-goal: A pie chart. The sectors are gauges, so they must read as arcs, not slices.

BOLD FLAT

07-bold-flat.html

Personality: Blunt and modern. Two strokes and a dot, nothing else earns its place.
Anti-goal: Skeuomorphism. No bezel, no dial face, no shine.

DIMENSIONAL DARK

08-dimensional-dark.html

Personality: Lit glass. Deep plum ground, a glowing spectrum arc.
Anti-goal: The current icon, whose 4px strokes vanish below 128px.

Pick by filename. Files are in docs/mockups/; the winner becomes assets/icon.html, which the build rasterises into the .icns.
